What colors should each condition show?

These will blend over your real skin tone — pick the palette that reads most naturally.

A

Clinical

Strong, high-contrast colors. Immediately obvious what's happening.

Acne — deep red
Redness — warm orange-red
Under-eye — purple
B

Natural / Subtle

Colors that could actually appear on real skin. Less alarming, more like what you'd see in a mirror.

Acne — crimson
Redness — soft rose

C

Heatmap

All conditions in the same warm-to-hot scale (yellow → orange → red). Severity = heat. Conditions distinguished only by position.
