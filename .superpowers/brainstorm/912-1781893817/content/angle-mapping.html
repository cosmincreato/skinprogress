How should the 3 angles map onto the 3D face?

Each photo covers a different region. Pick the mapping strategy that feels most accurate.

Face coverage per angle
■ Front — forehead, nose, chin, under-eyes
■ Left angle — person's left cheek, jaw
■ Right angle — person's right cheek, jaw
A

Zone Blending

Split the model into 3 zones (left cheek, center, right cheek) with soft Gaussian falloff between them. Each zone is colored by its angle's detections. Zones overlap at boundaries for a smooth transition.

Best fit: smooth, realistic. Most work.
B

Hard Split + Blend

Divide model at normX ≈ ±0.25: left third driven by left-angle detections, right third by right-angle, center by front-angle. A narrow blend band (~0.1 normX wide) smooths the seam.

Simpler, accurate zone ownership, minor visible seam risk.
C

Weighted Composite

Each vertex gets a weighted sum from all 3 angles. Weight = how well that angle "sees" the vertex (based on normX and normal direction). All angles contribute everywhere; dominance shifts by position.

Most physically accurate. Highest quality output.
